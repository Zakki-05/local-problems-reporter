About Pernambut Civic Hub

A digital bridge between citizens and the Pernambut Municipal Council for a cleaner, safer, and better city.

Our Mission

Pernambut Civic Hub (PGC) is a smart grievance reporting platform designed to empower the residents of Pernambut. Our goal is to streamline the communication between the public and the municipality, ensuring that every civic issue is heard, tracked, and resolved efficiently.

01

Transparency

Track the real-time status of your complaints from pending to resolved.

04

Community

View local issues on a live map and see how your neighborhood is improving.
